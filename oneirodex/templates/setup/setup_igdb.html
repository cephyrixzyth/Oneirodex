Optional. IGDB helps identify games and fill in metadata. You can skip it and choose other supported services in the next step.

How do I get IGDB credentials?
  1. Open the Twitch Developer Console and sign in.
  2. Register an application with the Website Integration category and an OAuth redirect URL of http://localhost.
  3. Copy its Client ID and create a Client Secret. Keep both private.

IGDB is owned by Twitch. You can safely finish setup without these credentials.
